Welcome to MicroSAM-LoRA! This application helps doctors and medical researchers accurately identify the prostate gland in micro-ultrasound images. It uses cutting-edge artificial intelligence to make segmentation (outlining the prostate in images) as straightforward as possible.

💡 Why Use MicroSAM-LoRA?

Prostate segmentation is a crucial step in diagnosing and treating prostate conditions. Traditional manual methods take time and are prone to inconsistency. MicroSAM-LoRA gives you two powerful approaches:

  • VGG16-UNet: An established convolutional neural network architecture known for reliable, efficient performance.
  • LoRA-fine-tuned MedSAM: Uses the latest parameter-efficient fine-tuning to leverage a powerful medical adaptation of the Segment-Anything Model (SAM). This approach often achieves higher accuracy with less computational demand.

The software automatically compares both methods, giving you insights into which one works best for your specific micro-ultrasound data.

🎮 How to Use MicroSAM-LoRA

Once the application is running, follow these simple steps:

  1. Load Micro-Ultrasound Images: Click the "Open" button or drag-and-drop your ultrasound images directly into the main window.
  2. Select a Model: Choose between "VGG16-UNet" and "LoRA-MedSAM" from the dropdown menu.
  3. Run Segmentation: Press the "Analyze" button. The AI will process your image and outline the prostate gland automatically.
  4. Compare & Save: If you'd like, run both models and use the side-by-side viewer to compare outputs. Export your results as PNG, JPEG, or TIFF with a single click.

🛠️ Troubleshooting and Support

Sometimes things don't go perfectly on the first try. Here are answers to common questions:

"The application won't start after download."

Make sure you have extracted the entire folder to one place if you downloaded a .zip file. Do not run the app from inside the compressed folder. Also, temporarily disable antivirus and try again — some security software incorrectly flags newer AI applications.

"My ultrasound images aren't loading."

Supported formats are DICOM (.dcm), PNG, and TIFF. Verify your files are either in one of these formats or unconverted. If using DICOM files, ensure the patient metadata is intact.

"The segmentation looks wrong."

Check that the input image is a proper micro-ultrasound frame. The model expects grayscale or lightly colored images. Heavily annotated or edited images may produce poor results.

If you continue to experience issues, report them on the GitHub repository page. The community and maintainers are active and responsive.
